Where the numbers and shapes come from

Chapters 1–2 use the exact archived asymmetric candidate shown in the Geometry Lab (evaluation 504, score 0.3452). The stiffness values come from its saved linear 3D FEM compliance matrices. Equal total normal loads are distributed over the two depth sections at the selected height. Displayed section translations are magnified 12×; the shown deformed surface is an interpolation of section translations, not a new nonlinear contact solve.

Chapter 3 is an original schematic of GET's two-against-one layout. Arrows illustrate force redistribution and a lever arm in one torque direction; they are not measured forces or a demonstration of 1 N·m capacity. Moving the grasp changes the V-shaped spacing. See Burgess & Adelson, GET (2025).

Chapter 4 shows six actual saved candidates. Transitions are cuts between archived shapes, not simulated material morphing. The latest candidate is a search outcome, not a proven optimum. Mirrored and rigid designs scored higher at this checkpoint. The separate GET-inspired search is not represented by these Fin-Ray shapes.
